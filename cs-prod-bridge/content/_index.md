# Computer Science Through Production Systems

{{< lead >}}A bridge from production experience to the computer-science models
behind scalable systems.{{< /lead >}}

This track starts from systems work: reconcilers, queues, indexes, caches,
dependency graphs, profiles, and incidents. It introduces formal models when
they make those systems easier to explain, predict, and debug.

It is not a LeetCode sequence or a credential. Each unit combines a rigorous CS
source, the relevant Go contract and implementation, a real production case,
runnable experiments, and report-first debugging scenarios.

# Evidence Packet 1: Snapshot records

Simplified local input, in arrival order:

```text
record ID: database-current
URN:       urn:database
state:     current

record ID: service-current
URN:       urn:service
state:     current
depends:   urn:database

record ID: database-old
URN:       urn:database
state:     pending deletion
```

The snapshot rules allow this current/old pair. The two database records are
different resources at different points in their lifecycles, even though they
have the same logical URN.

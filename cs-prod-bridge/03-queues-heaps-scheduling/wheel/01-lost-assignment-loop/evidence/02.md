Counters from two groups of 100 connected workers during one minute:

| Worker group | Acquire attempts | Jobs completed | Worker sessions retired | Assignments reported missing or superseded |

| existing workers | 48,920 | 0 | 0 | 48,920 |

The existing workers are not idle. They produce acquire traffic, but do not
release a slot or complete queued work.

These measurements are invented for the exercise. They separate activity from
useful progress without relying on a timing limit.

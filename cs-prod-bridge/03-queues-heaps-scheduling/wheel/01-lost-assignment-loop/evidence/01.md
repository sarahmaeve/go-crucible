# Evidence Packet 1: Recovery stages

The service team divided the incident into two observation windows:

| Window | Assignment-service capacity | New arrivals | Connected workers | Valid backlog |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10:00–10:10 | 35% | 1,200/min | 800 | 18,400 and rising |
| 10:10–10:20 | 115% | 180/min | 800 | 19,100 and nearly flat |
| 10:20–10:30 | 115% | 180/min | 800 | 2,300 and falling |

At 10:10, capacity expansion completed and new arrivals were throttled. At
10:20, the worker acquire-response policy changed. No queue-ordering or
assignment-service-capacity change was made at 10:20.

The figures are from the synthetic local scenario. The two-stage shape is
inspired by the public GitHub Actions incident report.

# Evidence Packet 1: Recovery stages

The service team divided the incident into three time windows:

| Window | Assignment-service capacity | New arrivals | Connected workers | Valid backlog |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10:00–10:10 | 35% | 1,200/min | 800 | 18,400 and rising |
| 10:10–10:20 | 115% | 180/min | 800 | 19,100 and nearly flat |
| 10:20–10:30 | 115% | 180/min | 800 | 2,300 and falling |

At 10:10, the service gained capacity and reduced new arrivals. At 10:20, the
worker's acquire-response rule changed. Queue order and assignment capacity did
not change at 10:20.

The figures are invented for this exercise. The public GitHub Actions incident
report inspired the two-stage pattern.

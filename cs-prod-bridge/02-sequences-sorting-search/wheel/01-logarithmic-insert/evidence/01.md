# Evidence Packet 1: Inputs and timing by phase

The same release runs in all regions. For 95 percent of measured operations,
the time was at or below the `p95` value.

| Region | Current records | Incoming records | Refresh p95 | Lookup p95 after ready |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| established-a | 42,100 | 180 | 240 ms | 38 microseconds |
| established-b | 44,800 | 230 | 310 ms | 40 microseconds |
| new-region | 41,900 | 12,400 | 18.1 s | 39 microseconds |

The publisher reports ready only after it publishes the completed snapshot in
one step. Query traffic starts afterward. Most keys in the new-region batch
belong before every key in the current snapshot.

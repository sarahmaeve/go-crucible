# Wheel of Misfortune Rubric

Score the investigation process separately from whether the final test passed.
For each dimension use 0–3: absent, emerging, competent, or strong.

| Dimension | Strong evidence |
|---|---|
| Report interpretation | Separates observed facts, reported claims, assumptions, and unknowns without silently strengthening any claim. |
| Clarification | Asks a small number of questions that materially narrow scope or competing explanations. |
| Hypothesis management | Keeps plausible alternatives alive and updates or discards them as evidence arrives. |
| Evidence selection | Chooses focused, relatively cheap evidence and predicts what different outcomes would mean. |
| Localization | Identifies the first local contract violation rather than repairing only the visible symptom. |
| Repair scope | Changes only what the evidence justifies and preserves neighboring behavior. |
| Verification | Reproduces the report, checks the repaired boundary, and runs an appropriate regression scope. |
| Communication | Gives a concise causal account, states certainty honestly, and respects the assigned component boundary. |

Do not award points for the sheer number of questions, commands, or
hypotheses. One discriminating comparison is better than an exhaustive
checklist. A lucky patch can score well on outcome and poorly on process.

Maximum score: **24**.

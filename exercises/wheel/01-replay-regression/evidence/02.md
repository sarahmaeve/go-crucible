# Evidence 02: Timeline and rollout

The application binary, deduplication configuration, metric schema, and retry
policy are identical in the legacy and Atlas pools. Traffic allocation changed
only the concrete `CacheStore` implementation.

Atlas had already served first-attempt writes for six hours without elevated
errors. The failure rate rose when a separate network event caused upstream
clients to replay previously accepted metrics.

The timing makes the store migration relevant, but does not by itself show
whether the adapter violates its contract or exposes an assumption in its
caller.

# Evidence 01: Scope and impact

The incident query covers the first 24 minutes after the retry burst began:

- Initial submissions through both cache stores succeeded.
- Failures occurred only when an already-submitted metric was replayed.
- Every sampled replay through Atlas surfaced an error to the caller.
- Sampled replays through the legacy store were reported as successful no-ops.
- No evidence shows a duplicate metric being published downstream; the visible
  symptom is the returned error.

The larger retry burst increased how often the latent path ran. It did not
establish why the two store paths produced different caller-visible results.

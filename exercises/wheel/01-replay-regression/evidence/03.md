# Evidence 03: Operation identity

For each sampled failure, the initial attempt and replay have identical metric
names and nanosecond timestamps. Those are the fields used to derive the local
deduplication key. Labels and values are also unchanged in the sample.

The key passed to `CacheStore.Put` is identical on the two attempts. The first
call succeeds; the replay receives the store's duplicate-key result.

There is no evidence in this sample of two distinct logical observations being
conflated under one key.
